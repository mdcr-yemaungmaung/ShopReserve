# Shop Portal Design System (Isolated)

This document contains the independent UI/UX Design System specifications for the EzBookNow **Shop Portal (Merchant Management Dashboard)**.

## Brand & Style
Designed specifically for restaurant and merchant operators. Focuses on operational speed, order/reservation management dashboards, table control, and metrics.

## Colors & Surfaces
- **Primary Navy Blue (`#131546`)**: Sidebar & main navigation context.
- **Merchant Lime Highlights (`#AAF457`)**: Status updates, active bookings, table layout status.
- **Surface Contrast**: Clean workspace cards with crisp boundaries and high legibility.
